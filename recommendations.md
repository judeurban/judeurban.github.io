# Tulsa Recommendations

A few favorite spots from Jessie & Jude for anyone visiting for the weekend.

## Good to Know
- If you have time, drive down 11th Street — historic Route 66. Lots of cute shops and fun touristy stops.
- Need groceries? Go to Trader Joe's or Aldi — Reasors is super overpriced.

## Friday
### Riverside & Gathering Place
Walk around Riverside and the Gathering Place — watch the river surfers and storks fishing!
- query: Gathering Place, Tulsa, OK
- category: activities

### SOBO District
Our urban kickoff spot! A super cute, very walkable district near the park where we'll grab ice cream.
- query: SOBO District, Tulsa, OK
- category: activities

### TXMZ
Mexican fusion — get the candied pork tacos.
- query: TXMZ, Tulsa, OK
- category: food

### Tina's
Smash burgers and cocktails, amazing either way.
- query: Tina's Burgers, Tulsa, OK
- category: food

### Mercury Lounge
Great spot for live music.
- query: Mercury Lounge, Tulsa, OK
- category: nightlife

## Saturday
### Pony Coffee
Coffee shops on Cherry Street aren't as good — this is only a 5 minute drive down the road and worth it. Good for kids as well.
- query: Pony Coffee, Tulsa, OK
- category: coffee

### Origin Coffee
Cool eclectic coffee shop in the middle of a quiet downtown neighborhood.
- query: Origin Coffee, Tulsa, OK
- category: coffee

### Swan Lake
Our favorite hidden gem — actual swans in the pond, and a super cute neighborhood to stroll around (Jude used to live here!).
- query: Swan Lake, Tulsa, OK
- category: activities

### Woodward Park
Beautiful and walking distance — some of it is under construction, but the upper half isn't.
- query: Woodward Park, Tulsa, OK
- category: activities

### Tulsa Farmers Market
Live music, great food, cute vendors. For breakfast, find the yellow food truck marked "elote" — great puffy breakfast tacos.
- query: Tulsa Farmers Market, Tulsa, OK
- category: activities

### Killer Whale BBQ
Owned by our awesome neighbors!
- query: Killer Whale BBQ, Tulsa, OK
- category: food

### Country Bird Bakery
Award winning bakery — worth standing in line for.
- query: Country Bird Bakery, Tulsa, OK
- category: food

### OutPour Coffee Market
More local Tulsa fun.
- query: OutPour Coffee Market, Tulsa, OK
- category: coffee

### Downtown Art Deco Stroll
Stroll around downtown and see all the beautiful art deco buildings and churches.
- query: Downtown Tulsa, OK
- category: activities

## Sunday

- category: food
